Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Data we handle

PostKu Scheduler runs locally on your device. Authorization tokens are stored in a local file on your device (tokens.json) and are used only to call the official content posting API on your behalf. Video files are sent directly from your device to the platform when you choose to post.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate a server that collects your personal data, messages, contacts, or browsing activity. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Retention and removal

Authorization can be revoked at any time from your account settings. You may delete the local token file and post history from your device at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information provided in the application registration.
